## Description (paste)

Everyday expense-line checker for contractors and small teams.

Email a claim plus a public receipt URL to ceilinggate-claims@agentmail.to. Convex stores the claim. Firecrawl reads the receipt. GRANT if every line is at or under the page. REFUSE names the overage in dollars. OpenAI writes one sentence after the numbers and does not decide.
